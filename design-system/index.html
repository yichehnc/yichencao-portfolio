04 / Shape, space & motion

  • 24px cards, pill-shaped actions, fine neutral borders and a very light shadow.
  • 48px minimum primary controls, a consistent 8px spacing rhythm, and readable mobile layouts.
  • 180ms hover feedback, 280ms one-time content reveals, and a subtle pointer glow on desktop.
  • Content stays visible without animation. Reduced-motion settings disable travel and decorative movement.
  • Keep the character illustrations, handwritten phrase and project imagery. Texture supports the content; it never covers it.
